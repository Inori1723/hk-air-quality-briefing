Methods and sources

District colors show the highest valid hourly AQHI among General stations in each district. Roadside readings appear in their own marker layer. The map distinguishes districts without General stations from districts where General stations had no valid observations.

Charts keep observation hours 01–24 in the original day. AQHI averages use numeric values 1–10. Serious readings are reported as 10+. Risk shares use valid hours, and valid substitute readings are counted separately. EPD updated the AQHI calculation method on 22 March 2025. AQHI changes across this date do not establish changes in pollutant concentrations.

AQHI and pollutant observations come from EPD. Weather is from the Hong Kong Observatory and population is from the Census and Statistics Department. The map uses the supplied Cydalytics district boundaries and the project's EPD station coordinates.
